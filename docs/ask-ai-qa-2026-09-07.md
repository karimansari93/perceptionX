> Nothing is missing — PerceptionX doesn't measure by month.
>
> Data is collected in periodic measurement waves, typically one per quarter, and reported by quarter. For Netflix the measured periods are Q1 2026, Q2 2026 and Q3 2026 — that's the full window, and all three are complete waves. May was never a separate measurement period, so there's no May row to be absent; anything AI platforms said in that timeframe sits inside the Q2 2026 wave, which is measured and final.
>
> A couple of related points worth knowing, since they can look like gaps but aren't:
>
> - Sample sizes differ between waves (e.g. Netflix UK has 6,022 answers across three periods, Netflix US 3,404). That's by design — always compare periods on percentages, never on counts.
> - Not every profile has the same number of waves. The 16 market profiles are all measured through Q3 2026, but Netflix House, Eyeline and the two Global profiles currently run through Q2 2026, and Netflix Animation Studios has two measured periods. So a brand-scope comparison to Q3 2026 covers the market profiles.
