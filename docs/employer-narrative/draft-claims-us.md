## Where the story changes by function

- **Ford, hybrid work:** in 88% to 90% of product development and research ads, but only 23% of manufacturing ads.
- **Ford, the future of transportation (electric, software):** in 59% of enterprise technology ads, but only 16% to 27% of supply chain, finance, and marketing and sales ads.
- **Netflix:** the US careers system lists almost no film and TV production roles. Its "Content & Production" figures come from games and Inkubator roles only (10 ads), so treat them as weak.

## Things worth knowing before matching

- **AI still cites old Ford and Netflix pages.** Ford's 2020 and 2021 sustainability reports appear in 185 and 62 answers. Netflix's 2021 inclusion report appears in up to 17 answers per URL variant. These are likely sources of out-of-date claims.
- **Not scanned yet:** newsroom and press releases. The brief lists them for v1, but this pass covers job ads and careers pages only.
